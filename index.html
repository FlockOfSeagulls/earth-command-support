How can we help?

Earth Command is an arcade missile-defense game for iPhone and iPhone Duo. Below is a quick guide and answers to the questions people ask most.

Need a hand? Email cliffordsiegel@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Tap the sky near a missile. The closest city with charge fires a laser at the spot you touched.
  • Aim ahead. The blast lingers for a moment, so tap where the missile is going.
  • Chain your blasts. A destroyed missile explodes and can take out its neighbors. Chain kills multiply your points.
  • Watch your cities. Each shot uses some of a city's charge. Cities in daylight recharge faster.
  • Shoot the capsules. Power-ups drift across the sky: Shield blocks the next hit, Overcharge makes lasers free and wider, and Slow Time slows everything down.
  • Three hits and it's over. Each wave is faster, with new threats: splitters from wave 2 and meteors from wave 3.

Common questions

I can't hear any music or sound effects.

Earth Command follows your iPhone's Ring/Silent switch, so it won't make noise when your phone is silenced. Turn Silent mode off, turn up the volume, and make sure the menu shows Sound on.

Why is the globe centered where it is?

The game centers Earth on a city in your iPhone's time zone, so you're defending your part of the world. Change your time zone in Settings and the view follows.

Is the day and night line real?

Yes. The game calculates where the Sun is overhead right now from your iPhone's clock and lights the globe to match. The menu shows the exact point under the Sun. Tap the Sun button on the menu to preview other times of day.

Why does it say "Recharging"?

Every city in range has spent its charge. Wait a moment, tap closer to a city that's ready, or grab an Overcharge capsule for free shots. Sunlit cities refill faster than cities on the night side.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Play folded on the outer screen or unfolded on the inner screen. The game adapts to portrait and landscape as you go.

I don't feel any vibration.

Haptics need an iPhone with a Taptic Engine and System Haptics turned on in Settings > Sounds & Haptics.

The game feels less smooth than usual.

Earth Command runs at up to 120 frames per second. Low Power Mode limits iPhone to 60, which is normal and still fully playable.

How do I reset my best score?

Your best score is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, and the game never connects to the internet. See the privacy policy.

Report a bug

Email cliffordsiegel@gmail.com with what happened, which wave you were on, and your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
