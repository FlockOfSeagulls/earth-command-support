Privacy policy

Your data stays yours

Last updated: November 8, 2026 (added Game Center)

The short version: Earth Command collects no personal data. There’s no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only time the app goes online is for Game Center leaderboards, which are optional and run by Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. Earth Command doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you or your device. We, Clifford Siegel ("we", "us"), have no servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

To work, the app uses a small amount of information that never leaves your iPhone:

  • Your time zone and clock. Used to center the globe on your region and to light it with the Sun's current position. This is read on the device and isn't stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your best score and sound setting. Saved in the app's local storage on your iPhone so they're there next time. They're removed if you delete the app. If you use iCloud or device backups, they may be included in those backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Game Center leaderboards

If you’re signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, Earth Command sends your final score to Apple’s Game Center at the end of each game, so it can appear on the monthly and all-time leaderboards next to your Game Center nickname. The app also asks Game Center for your current rank so it can show it to you.

Game Center is an Apple service, and Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy. We don’t receive or store your Apple ID, email address, or contacts. You can turn Game Center off, change your nickname, or hide your profile at any time in Settings > Game Center.

Network access

Apart from Game Center, the app makes no network requests. All graphics, music, and sound are built into the app.

Third parties

Earth Command contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store and crash statistics if you've chosen to share them with developers in your iPhone settings. That's controlled by you and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a future version ever needs to collect data, we'll say so here and in the App Store listing before it ships.
