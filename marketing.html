How it works

One tap. One laser.

Arcade missile defense, rebuilt around a living planet.

Tap the sky

Touch anywhere near an incoming missile. The closest city with charge fires a laser to that exact spot.

Lead your shot

The blast lingers for a moment. Aim where the missile is going, and catch two or three at once.

Hold the line

Every wave is faster and stranger. Three hits on Earth and the defense falls.

Live day and night

The real Sun, right now

The line between day and night is calculated from the Sun's actual position at this moment. City lights glow on the dark side, and the globe centers on your part of the world.

It matters, too: cities in sunlight recharge their lasers faster. Play at dawn and the battlefield looks, and plays, differently than at midnight.

Know your enemy

Every wave brings something new

  • Rockets. Steady, relentless, and everywhere.
  • Splitters. Violet warheads that break into three. Hit them early.
  • Meteors. Fast, heavy, and you'll hear them roar in. Lead your shot.
  • Close calls. A red ring and an alarm warn you when anything gets close.
Features

Small game. Big planet.

Power-ups

Shoot drifting capsules for a Shield, golden Overcharge lasers, or Slow Time.

Chain reactions

One blast can set off the next. Chain kills multiply your score.

Original soundtrack

A modern 8-bit score that speeds up wave by wave and turns to full alarm when you're one hit from defeat.

Feel every shot

Precise haptics for each laser, each chain, and each impact on Earth.

Silky smooth

Custom Metal rendering at up to 120 frames per second on ProMotion displays.

Monthly leaderboards

A fresh Game Center leaderboard every month, plus an all-time board. No sign-up beyond Game Center, no ads, no tracking.

Made for iPhone Duo

Play folded on the outer screen, or open it up and the battlefield stretches across the big display. Earth Command adapts to portrait and landscape.
